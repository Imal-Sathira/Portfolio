Employers Cake Ordering System

The Cake Ordering System allows employees to order cakes at a discounted manufacturing price, with a yearly limit of 12 kg per employee. Additionally, they receive a 1 kg free cake on their birthday, which must be claimed within three months. The system uses OTP-based login and notifies both the kitchen and security when an order is placed.
